About the company

Our milestones

Sastha Engineering was established in 1995 and is engaged in manufacturing precision turned parts for automobile, tractor and engine components.

The factory premises cover about 18,000 sq ft, with a built-up area of 16,000 sq ft across Unit 1 and Unit 2. Five engineers and three quality personnel look after process and quality control.

The proprietor has 30 years of experience in the field of engineering. We are confident of our ability to manufacture components to high quality standards and to meet customer production schedules on mutually agreeable terms.

Quality and inspection

Measured before it ships

Quality floor

Final inspection area and standards room, working under IATF 16949:2016 and ISO 9001:2015 certification by TUV.

Profile projector

Magnifies the outline of a part on a screen to check profiles, radii and angles.

Video measurement system

Camera based, non-contact measurement of small turned parts.

Trimos 2D height gauge

Measures heights, diameters and distances with high accuracy.

Roughness tester

Measures the surface finish of machined parts.

Height gauge

Checks heights and positions of features from a reference surface.

Air gauge

Checks bore and shaft diameters using air pressure, without touching the surface.

Slip gauges

Reference blocks used to set and check measuring instruments.

Bore dial gauges

Measure internal diameters and check taper and roundness of bores.

Pitch micrometer

Measures the pitch diameter of threads.

Digital micrometer

Digital reading of external dimensions.

Digital vernier

Quick digital reading of outside, inside and depth dimensions.

Refractometer

Checks the concentration of the coolant used on the machines.

Coordinate measuring machine (CMM)

Probe based measurement of complex dimensions, recently added to the factory.
